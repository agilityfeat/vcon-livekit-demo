How vCon Care works

One conversation, captured once and reviewable with role-appropriate detail.

📞

Automated intake calls

Patients join a live call with an automated intake assistant. The conversation is recorded and transcribed in real time, so nothing from the visit is lost.

🔐

Signed & encrypted records

Each call becomes a vCon record that is cryptographically signed and encrypted. The authoritative clinical transcript is verifiable, so you always know the record has not been tampered with.

👥

Role-based review

Doctors see the full record with audio and clinical detail; analysts see a de-identified, PHI-masked version of the same verified vCon. Every role gets exactly the view it needs.
